Mental Wellbeing, Sleep & Stress: When to Seek Professional Help

Stress, poor sleep, anxiety and low mood can affect concentration, appetite, energy and daily functioning. Persistent or severe symptoms deserve compassionate professional assessment rather than self-diagnosis.

Supportive routines

  • Keep a regular sleep and wake schedule.
  • Build gentle physical activity into the day when appropriate.
  • Maintain regular meals and hydration.
  • Stay connected with trusted people.
  • Reduce alcohol and avoid using medicines or substances to self-treat emotional symptoms.

Seek urgent help

  • Thoughts of self-harm or suicide
  • Severe confusion or sudden change in behaviour
  • A seizure or loss of consciousness
  • New severe neurological symptoms such as sudden weakness or difficulty speaking

When should you seek professional advice?

Consider a medical consultation when symptoms are persistent, recurrent, worsening, affecting daily life, or accompanied by unexplained changes in weight, appetite, sleep, bleeding, urination, breathing or general wellbeing. A qualified healthcare professional can decide whether examination, testing or referral is appropriate.
