Children's Health: Common Symptoms Parents Should Not Ignore

Children can become unwell quickly, and symptoms should be considered in the context of age, behaviour, hydration and breathing. Parents and caregivers should seek professional advice when concerned.

Watch for changes in

  • Breathing
  • Alertness and responsiveness
  • Fluid intake and urination
  • Persistent vomiting or diarrhoea
  • Fever and overall behaviour

Urgent warning signs

  • Difficulty breathing or blue/grey lips
  • A child who is unusually difficult to wake or not responding normally
  • Seizure
  • Severe dehydration or very little urine
  • A serious-looking rash with fever
  • Rapid deterioration or a caregiver's strong concern

When should you seek professional advice?

Consider a medical consultation when symptoms are persistent, recurrent, worsening, affecting daily life, or accompanied by unexplained changes in weight, appetite, sleep, bleeding, urination, breathing or general wellbeing. A qualified healthcare professional can decide whether examination, testing or referral is appropriate.
