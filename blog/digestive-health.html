Digestive Health: Indigestion, Gas, Bloating & Healthy Gut Habits

Digestive problems are common and may include bloating, gas, indigestion, acidity or heartburn, constipation, diarrhoea, nausea or abdominal discomfort.

Occasional digestive symptoms may be minor, but persistent, severe or changing symptoms should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.

Important: Digestive symptoms can have many different causes. Avoid self-diagnosing persistent symptoms and seek appropriate medical evaluation when needed.

Common Digestive Symptoms

Some commonly reported digestive symptoms include:

  • Bloating or abdominal fullness
  • Gas and frequent belching
  • Acidity or heartburn
  • Indigestion after meals
  • Constipation or loose stools
  • Nausea
  • Abdominal discomfort

Simple Habits for Better Digestive Health

1. Eat a Balanced Diet

Include a variety of nutritious foods in your diet. Individual food tolerance varies, so paying attention to foods that repeatedly trigger symptoms can be useful.

2. Stay Adequately Hydrated

Adequate fluid intake is important for overall health. Fluid requirements vary depending on factors such as climate, activity level and individual health conditions.

3. Eat Mindfully

Eating slowly and avoiding very large meals may help some people who experience indigestion or discomfort after eating.

4. Understand Your Food Triggers

Some people notice that particular foods or drinks worsen digestive symptoms. Keeping a simple food and symptom diary can help identify patterns.

5. Stay Physically Active

Regular physical activity is an important part of a healthy lifestyle and may support regular bowel habits.

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

Persistent or severe digestive symptoms should not be ignored. Medical evaluation is particularly important if symptoms are associated with warning signs.

Seek medical attention if you experience:
  • Severe or persistent abdominal pain
  • Repeated vomiting
  • Difficulty or pain when swallowing
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Blood in vomit or stool
  • Black or tar-like stools
  • Persistent diarrhoea
  • Signs of dehydration
  • Yellowing of the eyes or skin

Why Proper Evaluation Matters

Digestive symptoms can occur for many different reasons, including dietary factors, infections, functional digestive disorders and other medical conditions.

A healthcare professional can review your symptoms, medical history and other relevant factors and decide whether further examination or testing is appropriate.
