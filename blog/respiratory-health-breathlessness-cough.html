Cough, Wheezing & Breathlessness: Understanding Respiratory Symptoms

Cough and breathing symptoms can have many causes, including infections, asthma, allergies, smoke exposure and other lung or heart conditions. Persistent or severe symptoms deserve medical assessment.

Common respiratory symptoms

  • Cough
  • Wheezing
  • Shortness of breath
  • Chest tightness
  • Phlegm or mucus
  • Reduced exercise tolerance

What can help

  • Avoid tobacco smoke and second-hand smoke.
  • Reduce exposure to known dust, fumes or other triggers.
  • Stay hydrated unless a clinician has advised fluid restriction.
  • Use prescribed inhalers or other medicines exactly as directed.
  • Seek assessment if symptoms keep returning or interfere with daily activities.

When should you seek professional advice?

Consider a medical consultation when symptoms are persistent, recurrent, worsening, affecting daily life, or accompanied by unexplained changes in weight, appetite, sleep, bleeding, urination, breathing or general wellbeing. A qualified healthcare professional can decide whether examination, testing or referral is appropriate.
