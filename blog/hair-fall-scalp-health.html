Hair Fall & Scalp Health: Common Causes and When to Get Help

Hair shedding is common, but sudden, severe or persistent hair loss can have many causes. These may include recent illness, nutritional problems, hormonal changes, medicines, scalp disorders or inherited patterns.

When hair fall deserves attention

  • Sudden or rapidly increasing shedding
  • Bald patches
  • Scalp pain, redness, scaling or pus
  • Hair loss with marked fatigue or other new symptoms
  • Hair loss that continues for several months

Gentle hair-care habits

  • Avoid tight hairstyles that pull on the scalp.
  • Limit repeated harsh chemical treatments or excessive heat.
  • Eat a varied diet with adequate protein and nutrients.
  • Avoid starting high-dose supplements without appropriate advice.
  • A clinician can help identify whether blood tests or scalp evaluation are needed.

When should you seek professional advice?

Consider a medical consultation when symptoms are persistent, recurrent, worsening, affecting daily life, or accompanied by unexplained changes in weight, appetite, sleep, bleeding, urination, breathing or general wellbeing. A qualified healthcare professional can decide whether examination, testing or referral is appropriate.
