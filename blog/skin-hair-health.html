Healthy Skin & Hair: Common Problems and Everyday Care

Healthy skin and hair are influenced by nutrition, hydration, hormones, genetics, environmental factors and everyday habits. Understanding common symptoms can help you decide when routine self-care is appropriate and when professional evaluation may be needed.

Important: Skin and hair problems can have many different causes. Persistent, severe or rapidly changing symptoms should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying only on self-treatment.

Common Skin Concerns

Some commonly reported skin concerns include:

  • Dry or excessively oily skin
  • Acne and occasional breakouts
  • Itching or irritation
  • Rashes or changes in skin texture
  • Changes in pigmentation or skin colour
  • Recurring skin inflammation

Common Hair & Scalp Concerns

Hair and scalp problems can also occur for many reasons. Common concerns include:

  • Increased hair shedding
  • Thinning hair
  • Dandruff or scalp flaking
  • Dry or itchy scalp
  • Excessively oily scalp
  • Breakage or changes in hair texture

What Can Affect Skin and Hair Health?

Skin and hair can be affected by several factors, including:

  • Family history and genetics
  • Age and hormonal changes
  • Nutrition and eating patterns
  • Stress and inadequate sleep
  • Some medicines or medical conditions
  • Environmental exposure
  • Hair and skin-care practices

Everyday Skin-Care Habits

Simple habits can help support healthy skin:

  • Keep the skin clean without excessive washing or scrubbing.
  • Use a suitable moisturiser when the skin is dry.
  • Protect exposed skin from excessive sun exposure.
  • Avoid repeatedly using harsh or irritating cosmetic products.
  • Maintain adequate hydration and a balanced diet.

Everyday Hair-Care Habits

  • Avoid excessive heat and harsh chemical treatments.
  • Use gentle hair-care products appropriate for your scalp.
  • Avoid repeatedly pulling or tightly tying the hair.
  • Maintain a balanced diet containing adequate protein and essential nutrients.
  • Address persistent dandruff, itching or scalp inflammation rather than ignoring it.

When Should You Consult a Doctor?

Professional evaluation may be appropriate if you experience:

  • Sudden or significant hair loss
  • Bald patches or rapidly progressing thinning
  • Persistent or severe itching
  • Recurrent or spreading skin rashes
  • Painful, infected or rapidly changing skin lesions
  • Skin changes that do not improve or continue to worsen
Do not ignore unusual skin changes. A new, rapidly changing, bleeding or non-healing skin lesion should be assessed by a qualified medical professional.

Skin & Hair Health and Overall Health

Sometimes skin or hair changes may occur alongside other health concerns. For example, changes in hormones, nutritional status, stress or certain medical conditions may affect hair growth or skin appearance.

For this reason, persistent symptoms should be considered in the context of the person's overall health rather than treated as an isolated cosmetic issue.

Final Takeaway

Healthy skin and hair are supported by good nutrition, adequate sleep, appropriate hygiene, sensible skin and hair care, and attention to changes that persist or worsen.

If you have ongoing skin or hair concerns, professional evaluation can help identify possible causes and determine whether further assessment is required.
