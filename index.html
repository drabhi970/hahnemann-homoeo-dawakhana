Patient-Friendly Health Information

Digestive Health: Common Causes, Daily Habits and When to Seek Medical Care

Digestive symptoms such as acidity, bloating, constipation and loose stools are common. They may sometimes be related to diet and lifestyle, but persistent or severe symptoms can require medical evaluation.

Acidity and Heartburn

Heartburn can occur when stomach contents move back towards the food pipe. Eating large meals, lying down soon after eating and some foods may worsen symptoms in some people.

Bloating

Bloating may be associated with eating quickly, constipation, certain foods or other digestive conditions. Keeping a food and symptom diary can sometimes help identify patterns.

Constipation

Regular physical activity, adequate fluid intake, sufficient dietary fibre and a consistent toilet routine can support normal bowel function.

Simple Daily Habits

  • Eat regular, balanced meals.
  • Include appropriate fibre in your diet.
  • Drink adequate water unless medically restricted.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Avoid lying down immediately after large meals.
  • Pay attention to foods that repeatedly trigger symptoms.

When Should You Seek Medical Care?

Consult a qualified healthcare professional if digestive symptoms are persistent, recurrent or interfering with daily life.

Seek urgent medical attention for severe abdominal pain, vomiting blood, black or bloody stools, severe dehydration, fainting or other rapidly worsening symptoms.
