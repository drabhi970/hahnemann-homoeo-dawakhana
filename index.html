Digestive Health

Digestive Health: Common Causes, Daily Habits and When to Seek Medical Care

Digestive symptoms such as acidity, bloating, constipation and diarrhoea are common. Their causes can range from diet and lifestyle factors to infections, medicines and underlying medical conditions.

Helpful Daily Habits

  • Eat regular, balanced meals.
  • Drink adequate fluids unless medically restricted.
  • Include appropriate fibre in your diet.
  • Stay physically active.
  • Notice foods that repeatedly trigger symptoms.
  • Avoid unnecessary self-medication.

When to Seek Medical Care

Consult a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms are persistent, recurrent, worsening, or associated with unexplained weight loss, blood in the stool, persistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain or other concerning symptoms.
