Child Health & Learning

Dyslexia and Learning Difficulties in Children: Signs, Causes and Support

Dyslexia is a common learning difficulty that can affect reading, spelling and the connection between speech sounds and written words. Early identification and appropriate educational support can help children learn effectively.

Early signs

  • Difficulty learning new words or rhymes
  • Difficulty connecting letters with sounds
  • Problems with spelling and reading fluency
  • Difficulty remembering sequences or spoken instructions

What should parents do?

If learning difficulties persist, discuss them with a pediatrician, psychologist, developmental specialist or qualified educational professional. Structured reading instruction and individualized educational support may be recommended after assessment.

Role of Homoeopathy

Homoeopathic consultation should not replace educational intervention, developmental assessment or specialist care for dyslexia. There is currently insufficient reliable evidence that homoeopathic medicines can treat or cure dyslexia itself.
