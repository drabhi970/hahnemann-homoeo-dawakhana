Featured Health Article

Digestive Health: Common Causes, Daily Habits and When to Seek Medical Care

Educational information for patients and families.

Why digestive symptoms are common

Acidity, heartburn, bloating, constipation and diarrhoea can have many causes, including eating patterns, stress, medicines, infections, food intolerance and underlying medical conditions. Persistent or recurrent symptoms deserve proper evaluation.

Acidity and heartburn

Helpful general measures may include regular meals, avoiding very large meals, identifying personal triggers and avoiding lying down immediately after eating.

Constipation

Constipation may be related to low fibre intake, inadequate fluids, reduced activity, changes in routine or medicines. Gradually increasing fibre, adequate fluids when medically appropriate and regular physical activity can help many people.

When to seek medical care

  • Symptoms are persistent, recurrent or getting worse.
  • Unexplained weight loss, persistent vomiting or difficulty swallowing.
  • Blood in stool or black/tarry stool.
  • Severe or persistent abdominal pain.
  • Signs of dehydration or significant weakness.
Important: Severe abdominal pain, vomiting blood, black stools, fainting, severe dehydration, chest pain or difficulty breathing requires urgent medical assessment. This article is educational and does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.

Healthy daily habits

  • Eat a varied, balanced diet and include fibre-rich foods as tolerated.
  • Drink adequate fluids unless medically restricted.
  • Stay physically active according to your ability.
  • Maintain a regular sleep routine.
  • Do not stop prescribed medicines without medical advice.
